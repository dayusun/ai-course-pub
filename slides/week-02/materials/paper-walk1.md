# WALK-1: a brisk-walking programme for stage 1 hypertension (synthetic)

*Journal of Invented Trials, 2021. All content is fictional.*

**p. 3.** We randomised 240 adults with stage 1 hypertension at two community clinics to a
24-week supervised brisk-walking programme (n = 120) or usual care (n = 120).

**p. 5.** The primary outcome was change in office systolic blood pressure (SBP) at 24 weeks.

**p. 7.** At 24 weeks, mean SBP fell by 6.1 mmHg more in the walking group than in usual
care (95% CI 3.2 to 9.0). Adherence, defined as attending 70% of sessions, was 64%.

**p. 9.** Limitation: participants were recruited in 2018 and 2019, before the 2020
change in clinic referral rules.
