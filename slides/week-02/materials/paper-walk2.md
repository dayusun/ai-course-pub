# WALK-2: unsupervised walking with step counters (synthetic)

*Invented Health Reports, 2023. All content is fictional.*

**p. 2.** WALK-2 enrolled 410 adults (2020-2022) and gave the intervention group a step
counter and a weekly text message, without supervised sessions.

**p. 4.** The primary outcome was change in home SBP at 24 weeks.

**p. 6.** The between-group difference in home SBP was 2.4 mmHg (95% CI -0.3 to 5.1).
The authors describe the effect as "small and uncertain".

**p. 8.** 31% of the intervention group stopped wearing the counter before week 12.
